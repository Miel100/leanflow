LeanFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Effective: November 13, 2026

In short: we collect what we need to bill you and run LeanFlow for your factory. Your factory data is processed only to serve you — never sold, never used to train AI models. Pilot data is deleted 90 days after the pilot ends. You can ask us to access, export or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how ProofChain LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("ProofChain," "we," "us"), handles data in connection with LeanFlow — its website, software, 7-Day Pilot and hardware kits. ProofChain's other products have their own privacy notices.

Contact for privacy matters: mailframeapp@gmail.com · ProofChain LLC, 5830 E 2nd St, Ste 7000 #38324, Casper, Wyoming 82609, USA.

2. Our roles

  • Controller — for data about our customers and prospects (names, business emails, company and billing details, support messages) and for our own security and operational logs.
  • Processor — for personal data contained in factory data our customers provide to LeanFlow ("Customer Data"), such as operator names in production records. The customer is the controller; we process it only on their instructions to provide the service. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available on request.

3. What we collect

  • Contact and sales data — what you send through our contact form or by email: name, business email, company, the plan you are interested in, and your message.
  • Account and billing data — business name, contact name and email, billing and shipping address, and VAT or tax ID. Payments are handled by Stripe; we receive limited transaction details and never your full card number.
  • Customer Data — production orders, products and bills of materials, inventory, machine and sensor readings, energy meter data (for example utility "Green Button" downloads), working hours and tariff choices, and data imported from your ERP or other systems.
  • Access credentials — we store personal pilot links only as one-way hashes, never in readable form. Credentials you enter for a one-time system import (for example an ERP API key) are used for that import and not stored.
  • Technical data — IP addresses and request metadata used for security, rate limiting and troubleshooting.

5. AI features

Some LeanFlow features produce plain-language explanations — for example why a plan changed or what an energy alert means. To generate them we send summarized figures (such as machine identifiers, health and energy indicators, and schedule results) to an AI language-model provider acting as our subprocessor. We do not send your uploaded files, and we do not use Customer Data to train AI models. If no AI provider is available, LeanFlow falls back to standard explanations generated without one.

7. International transfers

We are a US company and LeanFlow runs on infrastructure in the United States. Where we transfer personal data of people in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where the recipient is certified) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep data

  • Pilot data (orders, meter data, settings, reports): deleted automatically 90 days after the pilot ends.
  • Energy and sensor readings for subscribers: kept on a rolling basis, typically up to 90 days of history, for forecasting and alerts.
  • Other Customer Data: kept while your subscription is active and deleted within 60 days after it ends, unless you ask for an export first.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically 7 years).
  • Contact-form and sales messages: up to 24 months after our last exchange.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you are (including under the GDPR and UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email mailframeapp@gmail.com. For personal data inside Customer Data, please contact the business that uses LeanFlow (the controller); we will help them respond.

You may also complain to a data protection authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali — or your local authority.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), pilot access links stored only as hashes, isolated pilot workspaces, access controls and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected customers of a personal data breach as the law requires.

11. Cookies

This website uses only what is necessary to work. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers. The pilot portal keeps your pilot link in your browser's session storage so the page keeps working when you refresh; it is cleared when you close the tab.

12. Children

LeanFlow is a business service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to notify customers.
